Our Story & Mission

We're engineering the foundation for
the world's fastest startups.

Founded in San Francisco by veteran distributed systems engineers and venture founders, Stackly was born to eliminate the painful friction between infrastructure operations and executive business intelligence.

Born out of frustration with infrastructure sprawl.

Before Stackly, engineering leads spent 30% of their sprints maintaining fragmented Terraform files, debugging silent microservice cascades, and fielding questions from executives about cloud hosting bills.

We asked a simple question: What if cloud orchestration were entirely autonomous, sub-millisecond telemetry were standard, and executive revenue growth were automatically synchronized with compute costs? That question became Stackly.
