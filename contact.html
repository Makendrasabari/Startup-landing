FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about migrating your startup infrastructure to Stackly.

How quickly can we migrate from AWS or GCP to Stackly?

Most seed to Series A startups complete migration within 48 hours using our automated cluster import CLI. We support live traffic mirroring so you can validate performance before cutting over DNS.

Does Stackly support SOC2 Type II compliance out of the box?

Yes. Stackly enforces mutual TLS 1.3 encryption across all internal services, automated key rotation, and continuous compliance audit logging. We provide pre-audited evidence packages for auditors.

Can both our Founders and DevOps engineers use separate views?

Absolutely. Stackly provides dedicated role portals: an Executive View focused on ARR, unit margins, and runway; and a DevOps Command Center focused on Kubernetes pods, API latency, and CI/CD rollouts.

What SLA uptime guarantees and credit compensation do you offer?

We guarantee a 99.999% high-availability uptime SLA across all production clusters. If availability drops below this threshold in any billing cycle, you receive automated pro-rated service credits applied directly to your monthly invoice.

How does Stackly pricing scale as our monthly active telemetry spikes?

Unlike legacy APM providers that penalize traffic surges with punitive overage fees, Stackly features predictable flat-rate node billing with intelligent adaptive sampling, eliminating surprise month-end cloud bills.
